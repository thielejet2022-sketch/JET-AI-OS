# RevOps Academy & Assessment Lab Scoring Model

**Version:** 1.0  
**Canonical status:** Approved  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-30

## Purpose

This specification defines the canonical scoring model used by the RevOps Academy & Assessment Lab to develop and evaluate demonstrated Revenue Operations capability.

The model is shared across both Academy Mode and Assessment Lab Mode. Academy work develops capability; Assessment Lab work measures demonstrated capability. The underlying competency and evidence standards remain unified unless a future approved change establishes a demonstrated need for separation.

## Source of Truth

- **Primary system:** GitHub, this specification.
- **Secondary systems:** ChatGPT Project instructions, Notion learner-facing materials, assessment outputs, and working notes.
- **Authoritative content:** Scoring dimensions, evidence standards, observation rules, interpretation rules, and mode boundaries defined here.
- **Update flow:** Material scoring-methodology changes must be explicitly approved by JET before this canonical specification is changed.
- **Conflict rule:** If a secondary system conflicts with this specification, this GitHub specification governs until an approved canonical change is committed.

## Core Scoring Principle

Score demonstrated capability from observable evidence, not title, credentials, reputation, confidence, or assumed experience.

Do not manufacture precision. If the available evidence does not support a defensible judgment, mark the competency or dimension **insufficiently observed** rather than forcing a score.

## Canonical Scoring Dimensions

The scoring model evaluates the following dimensions:

1. **Reasoning**  
   Ability to connect facts, patterns, constraints, and implications into a coherent analytical path.

2. **Questioning**  
   Ability to identify the questions that most efficiently reduce uncertainty and improve diagnosis or decision quality.

3. **Evidence Use and Evidence Discipline**  
   Ability to distinguish what is known from what is assumed, request relevant evidence, use evidence proportionately, and avoid conclusions that outrun the available facts.

4. **Prioritization**  
   Ability to identify what matters most, sequence investigation or action logically, and focus attention where it will produce the greatest decision value.

5. **Business Judgment**  
   Ability to interpret RevOps conditions in business context, recognize tradeoffs, anticipate consequences, and recommend actions appropriate to the operating situation.

6. **Communication**  
   Ability to present observations, uncertainty, analysis, recommendations, and implications clearly to the intended audience.

## Evidence Classification

Evaluator analysis must distinguish among the following evidence types rather than blending them together:

- **Observation:** A fact or pattern directly supported by the scenario or supplied data.
- **Hypothesis:** A plausible explanation that has not yet been established by evidence.
- **Evidence request:** Additional information needed to test a hypothesis, narrow uncertainty, or support a recommendation.
- **Inference:** A conclusion derived from available evidence, with its evidentiary basis made clear.
- **Recommendation:** A proposed decision, action, investigation, or priority based on the analysis.

These classifications are part of evidence discipline and should be reflected in scoring where relevant.

## Observation Standard

A dimension may be scored only when the exercise produces enough observable evidence to support a defensible judgment.

When evidence is inadequate, use:

**Insufficiently observed**

This status means the exercise did not produce enough evidence to judge the capability. It is not equivalent to weak performance and must not be treated as a low score.

## Academy Mode

Academy Mode prioritizes capability development.

The learner should ordinarily reason before receiving a complete solution when practical. Evaluator or instructor feedback may:

- identify strengths in the learner's reasoning;
- challenge incomplete reasoning;
- ask for deeper analysis;
- surface missed signals after the learner has engaged;
- explain stronger analytical paths;
- distinguish observations from unsupported conclusions;
- provide developmental recommendations.

Academy feedback may use the canonical scoring dimensions to make progress visible, but developmental coaching must not be confused with formal assessment evidence.

## Assessment Lab Mode

Assessment Lab Mode prioritizes fair, evidence-based measurement.

During a scored assessment:

- do not coach unless the exercise explicitly permits coaching;
- score only demonstrated evidence;
- separate observations, evidence, scoring, interpretation, and development recommendations;
- do not infer capability from résumé claims, titles, credentials, or reputation;
- use **insufficiently observed** when evidence is inadequate;
- preserve the applicable dataset version, exercise version, and methodology version for reproducibility.

## Scoring Precision

Version 1.0 establishes the scoring dimensions and evidence rules but does **not** impose a canonical numeric scale.

A numeric scale, weighting model, proficiency bands, aggregation method, or pass/fail threshold must not be invented or treated as canonical without a separately reviewed and approved methodology change.

This prevents false precision while the framework develops through actual use and evidence.

## Exercise Design Implications

Reusable exercises should be capable of exposing one or more canonical scoring dimensions through observable behavior.

Where practical, an exercise specification should identify:

- competencies or dimensions intended to be observed;
- evidence available to the participant;
- evaluator-only information, if any;
- permitted coaching behavior;
- applicable dataset and version;
- applicable rubric or scoring methodology version.

Exercises should reveal how a participant thinks rather than merely test terminology recall.

## Interpretation Rules

When evaluating performance:

1. Record the observable evidence.
2. Classify the evidence where relevant as observation, hypothesis, evidence request, inference, or recommendation.
3. Determine which scoring dimensions were actually demonstrated.
4. Mark unobserved dimensions as **insufficiently observed**.
5. Separate the evaluation itself from developmental recommendations.
6. Avoid greater scoring precision than the evidence can support.

## Change Control

This scoring model is canonical methodology for RevOps Academy & Assessment Lab.

Material changes to scoring dimensions, scoring-model structure, evidence standards, numeric scales, weighting, aggregation, proficiency thresholds, or interpretation methodology require JET's explicit approval before canonical promotion.

Historical assessments retain the methodology version used at the time of assessment.
